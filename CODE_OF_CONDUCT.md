# Code of Conduct

## Purpose

RAX Compute Gateway is committed to a welcoming, safe, and productive community. Everyone
participating in the project is expected to help create an environment where
people can collaborate across differences in experience, identity, and point of
view.

## Expected conduct

Participants should:

- communicate respectfully and focus criticism on ideas and behavior;
- give and accept constructive feedback in good faith;
- respect boundaries, privacy, and requests to stop unwelcome behavior;
- acknowledge mistakes, correct them, and learn from them;
- consider the safety and well-being of the wider community.

## Unacceptable conduct

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- harassment, threats, intimidation, stalking, or sustained disruption;
- discriminatory or demeaning comments, imagery, or unwanted attention;
- sexualized language, imagery, or advances;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- impersonation, deliberate deception, or retaliation against a reporter;
- conduct that a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a
  professional community.

## Scope

This code applies in repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, project
events, and project-related public or private communication. It also applies
when someone officially represents RAX Compute Gateway.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to
[pokemnn@yahoo.co.jp](mailto:pokemnn@yahoo.co.jp). Include links, dates, context,
and supporting material when it is safe to do so. Do not put sensitive personal
information in a public issue.

Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible, subject to
legal and immediate-safety obligations. Anyone with a conflict of interest must
step away from reviewing or deciding the report. Good-faith reporters and
witnesses must not be retaliated against.

For security vulnerabilities, use the separate process in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove content, request a correction, issue a warning, restrict
participation temporarily, or permanently exclude a participant. Responses
will consider severity, impact, history, and whether the behavior continues
after notice. Immediate restrictions may be used when necessary to protect the
community.

An affected participant may request one appeal by emailing the reporting
address within 14 days. When possible, an uninvolved maintainer will review the
appeal. The decision after appeal is final.
