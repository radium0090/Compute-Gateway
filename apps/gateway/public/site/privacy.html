Information we handle

We handle account and organization details, business inquiries, authentication and security events, API-key metadata, and bounded usage and reliability metadata. API keys, passwords, and session tokens are stored only as non-reversible hashes where the protocol permits it.

The gateway does not persist prompt, completion, or tool content by default. Requests are still transmitted to the model provider selected by the configured route so that provider can process them.

We use the information to respond to inquiries, verify and secure accounts, provide and support the service, enforce usage limits, diagnose reliability, prevent abuse, and meet legal obligations.

Hosting, transactional email, and the model providers configured for an account may process limited data on our behalf or as independent providers. Their locations and retention rules may differ. Private deployments can be discussed when a customer needs additional data controls.

Retention and choices

We retain account, inquiry, security, and operational records only as long as reasonably needed for the purposes above, dispute resolution, and legal requirements. Contact us to request access, correction, or deletion. Some records may need to be retained for security or legal reasons.
